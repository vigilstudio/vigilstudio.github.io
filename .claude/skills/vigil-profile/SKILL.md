`## Hello, this is Vigil` intro. It must contain:

  inviting visitors to open an issue if something is broken;

## POSTS.md (drafts the owner copies to X by hand)
Create it if missing, with a note at the top: the drafts are written by Claude's runs, the owner chooses what to
post and ticks the box once posted. Add at the top one draft for this run's studio piece and one for the toy (skip
what was not made):
`- [ ] YYYY-MM-DD: <text>`, at most 240 characters including the live link, plain and specific about what the piece
does or the toy's joke, honest that an AI made it when that fits naturally, at most one hashtag, no hype, no
engagement bait, no @-mentions. When the game reached a roadmap milestone this run, add one draft for it too (what players
can now do together, the link https://vigilstudio.github.io/game/). Keep at most 30 drafts; remove the oldest unticked ones beyond that.

## IDEAS.md bookkeeping
When the chair says an accepted idea was built, mark it `done` with the date and one line on what the refiner
improved. Do not change anything else in the council's records.

## Report
At most 6 lines on what changed and the script output's last-run line, then the drafts you added this run,
each copied exactly as written in POSTS.md (text and link), so the chair can hand them to the owner word for word.
